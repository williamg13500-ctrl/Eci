01 — Philosophy

Each stitch
is a little act
of resistance

Wearable Sculpture

Garments built like objects — weight, seam and surface treated as material decisions, not trims.

Textile Archaeology

Every panel is excavated from something that already lived: offcuts, dead stock, worn denim.

Surface Intervention

Bleach, paint, burn, patch, repair. The surface is where the argument happens.
