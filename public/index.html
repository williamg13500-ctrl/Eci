04 — A MATERIAL PHILOSOPHY

ECI / STUDIO NOTES

Each stitch is
a little act of
resistance.

Fabric keeps time the way trees keep rings. Every panel is excavated from something that already lived: offcuts, dead stock, worn denim. The work is to listen to that history — then make a new one.
